Teaching and knowledge transfer

Understanding instead of memorising

For me, good teaching starts not with the slide but with the question of why a subject is difficult to understand. I break complex relationships into manageable steps, connect theory with practical examples and make room for questions.

As a lecturer at Aalen University, I teach cost and performance accounting. I also bring experience in mathematics, technical knowledge transfer and the content and visual improvement of university courses.

Subject and teaching focus

Cost and performance accounting

Application-oriented teaching of relationships, calculation methods and decision logic for university students.

Mathematics

Clear derivations, systematic methods and focused support for common conceptual difficulties.

Teaching materials

Structuring content logically and aligning exercises, explanations and visual presentation.

Audience-appropriate teaching

Adapting technical depth and language to the programme, prior knowledge, learning objective and available time.

Possible formats

  • External teaching assignments and individual courses
  • Support with tutorials and exam preparation
  • Revision of existing lecture materials
  • Development of understandable exercises and solution paths

For a reliable assessment, I need the topic, target group, scope and planned timeframe.
