Individual mathematics tutoring

Finding gaps in understanding, building transparent solution paths and regaining confidence in mathematics.

School and university

Tutoring that leads to independent problem solving

Mathematical difficulties often do not start with the current exercise but with an earlier gap in understanding. Together, we identify the unclear step, the missing foundation and the explanation that fits how you learn.

My goal is not to solve exercises for you. I show you a structured approach that helps you handle similar problems increasingly independently.

What we can work on

Understanding mathematics

Breaking concepts, rules and calculations into manageable steps and using suitable examples.

Analysing errors systematically

Finding the point where a solution path went wrong instead of only correcting the final answer.

Preparing for exams

Assessing current knowledge, prioritising topics and practising with a realistic plan.

Developing learning strategies

Finding methods to structure tasks, monitor progress and continue through difficult subjects.

Who the tutoring is for

I support school and university students, particularly in mathematics. Other technical or non-technical subjects can be assessed individually depending on topic and level.

  1. Clarify the starting point: You describe the subject, level, topic and learning goal.
  2. Check understanding: We identify where the actual difficulty lies.
  3. Practise deliberately: Explanations, exercises and repetition are tailored to your needs.
